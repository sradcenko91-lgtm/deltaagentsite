📐 Delta Shapes Architecture: Spot vs Curve vs Bid-Ask

How shaped concentrated liquidity operates under the Delta Liquidity infrastructure.

⬛

Spot (Uniform)

Capital is distributed with flat, identical depth across all bins throughout the chosen price corridor.

  • 🛡️ Lowest risk of price exiting range abruptly
  • 🧘 Zero-maintenance passive fee harvesting
  • 📊 Optimal for stable pairs (USDG/WETH, tokenized bonds)
🔔

Curve (Gaussian Peak)

Concentrates 70%+ of deposit density at the middle bins directly surrounding the current market price.

  • 🚀 2.5x to 4.2x capital efficiency & fee velocity
  • 📈 Designed for trending assets (NVDA, SPCX, SPY)
  • ⚡ Delta's flagship algorithm for active DeFi yield
⚡

Bid-Ask (Bimodal Outer)

Weights liquidity towards outer perimeter bins, designed to automatically buy dips and sell into rallies.

  • 💥 Captures explosive fee spikes during volatility shocks
  • 🐸 Ideal for high-beta tokens and meme pairs (Ponsan, ZZZ)
  • 🔄 Auto-accumulates lower, auto-takes profit higher
